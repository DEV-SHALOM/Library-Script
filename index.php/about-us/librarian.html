University Librarian’s Welcome

The academic heartbeat of Nigeria’s premier private university—serving students, faculty, researchers, and our wider community.

Prof. Lucky Tijani Abdulsalami
University Librarian

It is with great pleasure that I welcome you to the official website of the Igbinedion University Library, Okada. As the academic heartbeat of Nigeria’s premier private university, our library plays a pivotal role in supporting teaching, learning, research, and innovation within the university community and beyond.

The Igbinedion University Library is a modern, dynamic, and user-centered knowledge hub designed to meet the diverse information needs of students, faculty, researchers, and external scholars. Our physical and digital collections span a wide range of academic disciplines and include thousands of books, journals, reference materials, electronic databases, e-books, and open access resources.

Our mission is to empower users with timely, accurate, and relevant information while fostering an environment that promotes independent learning, academic excellence, and lifelong intellectual development.

Key Resources and Services Include:

  • OPAC: Seamlessly search our holdings from anywhere.
  • Digital Library Access: Thousands of e-journals, e-books, and databases.
  • Reference & Information Services: Personalized guidance and research support.
  • Current Awareness Services: Stay informed with updates and trending topics.
  • Reprographic & Wi-Fi: Convenient photocopying, printing, and internet access.

Our library staff is committed to delivering excellent service and assisting all users in navigating today’s complex information landscape. Whether you're a first-year student or a postgraduate scholar, the library is here to support your academic journey.

Warm regards,
Prof. Lucky Tijani Abdulsalami
