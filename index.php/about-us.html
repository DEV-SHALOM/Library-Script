About The Library

Igbinedion University, Okada, runs a Collegiate System that operates from 3 (three) campuses. The campuses are: The Main Campus; The College of Health Sciences Campus; and the Crown Estate. In line with this structure, the University Library has also adopted the collegiate system in administering the library.

The Colleges are as follows:

      College of Law
      College of Arts & Social Sciences (CASS)
      College of Business & Management Sciences (CBMS)
      College of Engineering
      College of Health Sciences
      College of Natural & Applied Sciences
      College of Pharmacy

Each of the College has a vibrant, well stocked Library. In addition to the College Libraries, there is also a Department of Nursing Library as well as the Alumni Centre Library at the Crown Estate which is being used as a Reading Room as at present.

In all of this, it is necessary to observe that the Central Library acts as the administrative and technical headquarters of the University Library system. It is the seat of the University Librarian from where he coordinates all other libraries in the system. All materials are acquired and processed centrally in the Central Library and thereafter they are distributed to the other satellite libraries according to the nature and type of materials.

Mission And Vision

Mission Statement Our mission is to facilitate teaching/learning process, research and discovery for our students, faculty and staff by offering collections in all formats and multiple copies wherever such information resources can be found; and by establishing services and learning environments that are responsive to our user needs.

Vision Statement Igbinedion University, Okada, (IUO Library) serves as the intellectual power house of the university community. Consequently it will continue to promote knowledge, research and intellectual discourse by acquiring, organizing and making scholarly resources available and accessible to all, on our campuses; ensure that the resources available are used effectively through the teaching of library information skills programmes; and provide generous and conducive spaces for study and discovery.
