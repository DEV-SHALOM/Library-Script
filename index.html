Circulation Hall

The heart of the Igbinedion University Library system where students access, interact, and discover resources daily.

Engineering Library

This dedicated study and reference zone is tailored for engineering students, providing technical texts, resources, and a quiet reading space.

NAS Library

The NAS Library (Natural and Applied Sciences) provides students and researchers access to a vast collection of science-focused resources and journals, all in a calm, well-lit study environment.

E-Library Computer Section

The digital access zone for students to explore online journals, databases, research materials, and e-learning platforms. Equipped with modern computers and high-speed internet.

Exhibition Hall

This vibrant exhibition space showcases academic achievements, university milestones, and student activities. A place where knowledge, creativity, and tradition meet in one gallery.

Binding Department

The Binding Department handles the finishing touches for academic documents and archival records. Precision tools and skilled technicians ensure long-term durability of important materials.
